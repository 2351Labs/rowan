# Bulk confirm

Admin tables confirm Flag and Assign with `rowan-dialog` in `alert` mode. The
toolbar keeps density and filters. The bulk bar owns the selection count.

1. Select rows.
2. **Flag** or **Assign driver**.
3. Confirm or cancel in the alertdialog.

Do not invent a second confirm widget. `rowan-dialog` `alert` already removes
the close control and uses `role="alertdialog"`.
